Garden & Estate

Outdoors

A lovely terrace, shaded by an awning and with a large extendable table, is perfect for outdoor meals and aperitifs facing the sea. It opens onto a 298 m² shaded private garden, fully enclosed and with automatic irrigation, with plenty of cool spots in high summer. Sunbeds with cushions are provided, and the garden shed holds chairs, 5 deckchairs, 2 extra sun loungers and a beach umbrella. There is also space in the garden to store electric bikes safely.

The estate

A secure residence with an on-site manager. Swimming pool with relaxation area, 3 tennis courts with sea views, pétanque, children’s play area and a club house with restaurant (traditional cuisine, homemade pizzas, fresh bread and pastries to order). Woodland walks start right from the estate.
