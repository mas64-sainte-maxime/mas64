The house

Welcome to Mas 64, a warm and cosy 3-star classified holiday home in the secure Collines de Guerrevieille residential estate in Sainte-Maxime. Set in the first row of the estate, it enjoys stunning views over the Gulf of Saint-Tropez, surrounded by Provençal nature, just minutes from the beach, the town centre and Beauvallon golf course.

Read more

Private garden and outdoor spaces

A lovely terrace, shaded by an awning and with a large extendable table, is perfect for outdoor meals and aperitifs facing the sea. It opens onto a 298 m² shaded private garden, fully enclosed and with automatic irrigation, with plenty of cool spots in high summer.

Read more

The estate and its activities

A secure residence with an on-site manager. Swimming pool with relaxation area, 3 tennis courts with sea views, pétanque, children’s play area and a club house with restaurant (traditional cuisine, homemade pizzas, fresh bread and pastries to order). Woodland walks start right from the estate.

Nearby

Guerrevieille beach and its restaurant are just a few minutes away by car or e-bike, and access to the estate’s private beach is available as a paid option. Beauvallon golf course is right next door. The centre of Sainte-Maxime, with its markets and harbour, where boat shuttles leave for Saint-Tropez, is also close by.
